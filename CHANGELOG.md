# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-20)

First release.

- User-space driver for the EM2750 + MT9M001 camera over libusb-win32: 1280×1024 RGGB at 15 fps.
- Viewer: live view, zoom/pan, auto exposure, white balance, snapshots (PNG/TIFF/JPG) and MP4
  recording.
- Windows installer with a signed libusb-win32 driver package, plus Start-menu entries for the app,
  "Repair camera driver" and the user guide; the uninstaller removes the driver.
